About

Hi! My name is Cadence, and I'm an interdisciplinary game developer.

Currently working full-time on an indie fighting game as the sole programmer and gameplay designer. If you know, you know.

Mostly a a solo developer, but I'm always looking for 2D artists to collaborate with on game jams and commercial projects. Email me your portfolio and let's make a game!

Game Development

I make indie games! You can find most of my projects hosted on itch.io, but I also released a free game on Steam in 2018.

Exfoliation, a fast-paced microgame collection about body dysmorphia, came first place overall out of 166 entries in the DJAM 8! The game was exhibited at the Queerness and Games Conference in 2025.

I volunteered with Arcade Commons on the Nightmare Kart arcade cabinet, contributing lighting programming and design. You can play it at Wonderville in Brooklyn, NY.
